Our Inspiration & Local Focus

CyberShield was created in response to the growing threat of online scams in Tanzania, where people risk losing their hard-earned money and personal information. Our goal is to make digital safety accessible to everyone through simple security education and local scam awareness supporting both English and Kiswahili.

Inspired by pioneers like Marcin Kleczynski (founder of Malwarebytes), who started young by solving real security problems, CyberShield aims to grow into a trusted global cybersecurity company starting right here in East Africa.

Quick Safety Tips

  • ✓ Never share your PIN, password, or OTP with anyone.
  • ✓ Be careful with unknown links and attachments in SMS or WhatsApp.
  • ✓ Always check the sender's phone number or official email address.
  • ✓ When in doubt, stop, verify, and check with CyberShield.
